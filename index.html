01 · The problem

Owning a home feels out of reach. Building one feels even harder.

Rising costs, finding land, designing a home and securing financing. It’s hard to know where to begin, or what you can actually afford.

02 · Fragmented

Too many specialists, no one with the whole picture.

Land, zoning, architects, engineers, contractors, suppliers, lenders, permits and utilities. A family can work through all of them before learning whether the home is achievable.

03 · Late surprises

Cost arrives after you’ve fallen in love.

Projects die when the numbers finally catch up with the drawings. Affordability should shape the design from the beginning, not show up at the end.

04 · Starter homes

Attainable shouldn’t mean stripped down.

Explore a home you love, make it your own, see what it might cost, and understand the steps from blueprint to keys. One connected experience, with the assumptions shown.

Why

Three reasons, none of them simple

Supply

Building fell behind demand

Underbuilding, restrictive zoning, minimum lot sizes and longer permitting limited new housing.

Cost

Building got more expensive

Construction has shown no long-run productivity growth since 1987, while land, labor and material costs rose.

Size

New homes grew 40% larger

Part of the increase buys more space. The starter home most families need largely stopped being built.
